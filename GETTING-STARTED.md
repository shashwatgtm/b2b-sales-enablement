# Getting Started with B2B Sales Enablement

## First 5 Minutes

### Test 1: Build a Quick Deck

Upload any PPTX file from your company (even a 2-slide template) and say:

"I have a meeting with [prospect name] tomorrow. They are a [industry] company evaluating [your product category]. Build me a 5-slide pitch using the style from my uploaded deck."

What you should see:
- Brand colors extracted from your upload
- A 5-slide deck in YOUR visual identity
- Problem-first narrative (not feature-first)
- Speaker notes for each slide

### Test 2: Get Competitive Ammo

"Build me a battle card against [your top competitor]. We keep running into them in [your market]. The main objection we hear is [their strongest argument]."

What you should see:
- Honest strengths and weaknesses (not propaganda)
- 5-7 landmine questions to ask prospects
- Objection handling with proof points
- Feature comparison focused on decision-driving capabilities

### Test 3: Prep for a Meeting

"I have a call with [Name], [Title] at [Company] at [time]. It's a [first/second/third] meeting. Last time we discussed [topic]. They are also looking at [competitor]."

What you should see:
- Attendee research with LinkedIn insights
- Context from previous interaction
- 3 discussion points tailored to the attendee
- A researched meeting starter (not generic)

## Tips for Best Results

**Upload a sample file.** The brand extraction works with any PPTX or DOCX  - conference decks, old proposals, even a company template. The more slides or pages, the better the extraction. Minimum: 3 slides or 2 pages.

**Name the prospect.** The skills research the company and attendees via web search. "Build a deck for Acme Corp" produces much better output than "build a deck for a logistics company."

**State the problem, not the feature.** Instead of "make slides about our API integration," say "the prospect's biggest pain is connecting their 14 spreadsheets to one dashboard." The skill builds around the pain.

**Give a slide/page count.** "5 slides" or "3 pages" constrains the output to the right density. Without this, the skill defaults to longer output.
